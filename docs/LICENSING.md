# Licensing

EASI View uses the same source-available community/commercial model as EASI JS.
The binding terms are in [LICENSE](../LICENSE).

Free community eligibility includes individuals for qualifying personal,
educational and research use; qualifying independent public/nonprofit academic
and research institutions; qualifying nonprofits; and organization groups with
annual gross revenue **strictly below USD 5,000,000**. The institution/nonprofit
exceptions are not subject to the revenue cap under the conditions in LICENSE.

Group revenue includes controlled and commonly controlled affiliates worldwide.
It is revenue, rather than profit, valuation or funding. At or above the threshold,
organizations without another eligibility exception require a written commercial
license. The license preserves the same 30-day nonproduction evaluation and
90-day eligibility transition provisions as EASI JS.

eligibility terms and notices. EASI JS and other third-party components retain
their independent licenses; the build includes those texts with the application.

This project has not published an npm package or a public repository as part of
its local alpha setup. Publication should follow the conformance, notice and
